# Security policy

This policy covers every Forest Integrations repository and product: Ranger, Affinity, Attunement, Lantern, Prospect, Sift, Bloom, Red Card and forestintegrations.com.

## Reporting a vulnerability

Email **security@forestintegrations.com** with what you found, where (product and version, or the page address), the steps to reproduce it and the impact. Please do not open a public issue or post the details until a fix has shipped.

## What happens next

- We acknowledge your report within 3 business days.
- We send a first assessment within 10 business days: whether we can reproduce it, how serious it is and what happens next.
- We keep you updated until the fix ships, and credit you if you would like to be named.

## Safe harbor

Do not access, change or delete data that is not yours, degrade a service for others, or test an organization's systems without its permission. Research that stays within that and is done in good faith will not be pursued.

The full policy is at https://forestintegrations.com/security/ and the contact is also published at https://forestintegrations.com/.well-known/security.txt.
